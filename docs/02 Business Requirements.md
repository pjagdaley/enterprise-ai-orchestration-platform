# 1. Purpose

This document defines the business requirements for the Enterprise AI Orchestration Platform (EAOP).

It identifies the business challenges, strategic objectives, stakeholders, business capabilities, scope, constraints, and success criteria that justify the development of a production-ready enterprise AI platform.

These business requirements establish the foundation for the functional requirements, solution architecture, implementation roadmap, and overall product strategy.

---

# 2. Business Problem Statement

Modern enterprises operate across numerous business applications, cloud platforms, collaboration tools, and enterprise knowledge repositories.

Employees spend significant time searching for information, switching between applications, coordinating manual processes, and performing repetitive knowledge-intensive work.

Traditional enterprise search and AI assistant solutions can retrieve information but generally cannot:

- Understand complex business intent.
- Coordinate multiple business activities.
- Execute intelligent workflows.
- Integrate seamlessly with enterprise systems.
- Provide governed and explainable AI-assisted decision support.

Organizations require an intelligent enterprise platform capable of orchestrating AI capabilities, enterprise knowledge, business workflows, and enterprise systems while maintaining enterprise-grade security, governance, operational control, and responsible AI practices.

---

# 3. Business Vision

To establish a centralized Enterprise AI Orchestration Platform that enables intelligent collaboration between employees, AI services, enterprise knowledge, and business systems to improve productivity, decision-making, operational efficiency, and enterprise innovation.

---

The project assumes:

- Organizations are strategically adopting AI.
- Enterprise knowledge exists across multiple repositories.
- AI complements rather than replaces human expertise.
- Users require trustworthy and explainable AI.
- Enterprise integrations will continue to increase.
- Responsible AI practices are organizational priorities.

## Out of Scope

- Fully autonomous business decision-making without human oversight
- Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) implementation
- Customer Relationship Management (CRM) functionality
- Custom foundation model training
- Industry-specific business processes beyond demonstration scenarios
- Business Intelligence reporting

# 16. Business Value Proposition

The Enterprise AI Orchestration Platform delivers business value by combining enterprise knowledge, AI capabilities, workflow automation, and enterprise integrations into a unified, reusable platform.

Unlike traditional enterprise search solutions or standalone AI assistants, EAOP provides intelligent orchestration across knowledge, workflows, business systems, and AI capabilities, enabling organizations to accelerate digital transformation while maintaining governance, security, operational control, and responsible AI practices.
