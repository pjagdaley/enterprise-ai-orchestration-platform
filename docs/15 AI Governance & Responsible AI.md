# 1. Purpose

The AI Governance & Responsible AI Framework establishes the enterprise governance model for the design, development, deployment, operation, and continuous improvement of Artificial Intelligence capabilities within the Enterprise AI Orchestration Platform (EAOP).

The framework defines the principles, policies, standards, controls, roles, and governance processes required to ensure that Artificial Intelligence is trustworthy, secure, explainable, accountable, and aligned with business objectives throughout its lifecycle.

Rather than governing only foundation models, this framework governs the complete AI ecosystem, including:

- Foundation Models
- Prompt Engineering
- AI Agents
- LangGraph Workflows
- Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)
- Enterprise Knowledge
- Model Context Protocol (MCP)
- Enterprise Tool Execution
- Human Oversight
- Operational Monitoring
- Continuous Evaluation

The framework supports responsible adoption of enterprise AI while ensuring compliance with security policies, organizational governance, architectural standards, and emerging regulatory expectations.

The intended audience includes:

- Enterprise Architects
- Solution Architects
- AI Architects
- AI Engineers
- Platform Engineers
- Security Architects
- Risk & Compliance Teams
- Business Owners
- DevSecOps Engineers
- Operations Teams

This document establishes the enterprise governance foundation for all AI capabilities delivered by the Enterprise AI Orchestration Platform.

# 3. AI Governance Principles

The Enterprise AI Orchestration Platform adopts a governance model that treats Artificial Intelligence as an enterprise capability requiring the same level of governance as security, data, and enterprise architecture.

The following principles guide all AI implementation and operation.

---

## Human Accountability

Human decision makers remain accountable for AI-assisted outcomes.

Artificial Intelligence supports human decision making but does not replace organizational accountability.

Responsibilities shall always remain attributable to identifiable business owners.

---

## Transparency

Users should understand:

- When AI is being used
- Which knowledge sources contributed
- Which tools were invoked
- Which workflows executed
- What limitations exist

Transparency improves trust, adoption, and governance.

---

## Explainability

AI responses should be explainable whenever practical.

Explainability may include:

- Source citations
- Knowledge references
- Workflow traceability
- Agent execution history
- Tool invocation records
- Decision rationale

Explainability supports operational confidence and regulatory compliance.

---

## Security by Design

Security shall be incorporated into every AI capability.

Security controls include:

- Authentication
- Authorization
- Encryption
- Secret management
- Secure tool invocation
- Audit logging
- Secure deployment

---

## Privacy by Design

Artificial Intelligence shall respect enterprise privacy requirements.

Privacy controls include:

- Data minimization
- Access control
- Sensitive information protection
- Secure storage
- Retention management
- Controlled disclosure

---

## Responsible AI

Every AI capability shall be designed to be:

- Reliable
- Safe
- Explainable
- Governed
- Accountable
- Secure

Responsible AI principles shall guide both implementation and operational governance.

---

## Continuous Evaluation

Artificial Intelligence is not considered static.

Models, prompts, workflows, retrieval quality, and enterprise knowledge shall be evaluated continuously to maintain quality and operational effectiveness.

---

## Least Privilege

AI agents shall operate using the minimum permissions required for their approved responsibilities.

Tool execution shall be explicitly authorized.

Knowledge access shall be restricted according to enterprise policies.

---

## Governance by Default

Governance controls shall be integrated into platform design rather than introduced after deployment.

Governance applies throughout:

- Development
- Testing
- Deployment
- Operations
- Continuous improvement
